Page 02 · Study design

From two clinic visits to one clear question.

Can baseline measurements recorded while a patient is prediabetic identify who goes on to develop Type 2 Diabetes at follow-up?

Five follow-up cohorts

Outcome definition (verified against data)

progressed_to_t2d

Baseline visit (timed = 1) with type_3 = 1 (prediabetes) joined by patientid to the follow-up visit (timed = 2).

1 if follow-up type_3 = 2 (Type 2 Diabetes), otherwise 0.

Only baseline values are used as predictors, so follow-up measurements cannot leak into the model.
